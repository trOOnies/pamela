          Welcome to Pamela

          Your food and fitness diary, under a sun hat. 🌞

          Pamela is your personal food and fitness diary. Each day you can log what you eat, how you move, how you slept and how you feel, and then look back over weeks and months to see how it all adds up.

          🏠 It's all yours, on your computer. Pamela runs on your own machine and keeps everything in a single file, local/pamela.sqlite3. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. The only time it goes online is when you look up a barcode, and then only the barcode itself is sent.

          💾 No save button needed. Everything you add or change is saved the moment you do it.

          🗺️ Finding your way. The tabs at the top are the parts of the app:

          • Day: your diary for one day. This is where you'll spend most of your time.
          • Foods: your own list of foods and their nutrition.
          • Saved meals: combos you eat often, ready to log in one go.
          • History: charts and totals over 7, 30 or 90 days.
          • Settings: your targets, profile and exercise types.
          • Help: this page. Press ? to come back any time.

          ☀️ Light or dark? The sun / moon button next to Exit switches between the two. Pamela starts out matching your system's setting, and remembers your choice in this browser.

          When you're done, press 🚪 Exit in the top right corner to close Pamela properly.

          📅 Day

          This is where you log your day: what you eat, how you move, and how you feel. Use the arrows or the date at the top to change day, or press [ and ]. Dates are shown as year-month-day. The Today button is highlighted when you're on today, and days in the future can't be opened yet.

          🍽️ Logging a food

          1. Pick the meal. It already guesses one from the time of day.
          2. Start typing the food's name, brand or barcode, then choose it with ↑ ↓ and Enter.
          3. Type how much you had and press Enter. The line under the form shows what you're adding.

          🥄 Had only part of it? If the food has a serving or package size, tap 1 serving, ⅙ pack, ⅓ pack, ½ pack and so on. You can always type any amount.

          🆕 Not in your foods yet? Pick Create (or Look up barcode) at the end of the list, and the Foods tab opens with it filled in.

          ⭐ Saved meals

          Switch to Saved meal and pick one. Its foods show up as a draft: change amounts, remove things, add extras. Nothing is logged until you press Log, and the saved meal itself stays exactly as it was.

          ⚡ Quick add

          For one-offs, like a restaurant dish: type a name and the calories and macros you know.

          ✏️ Fixing the log

          Click an amount to change it (Enter saves, Esc cancels). + add logs straight into that meal, ☆ save keeps its foods as a saved meal, and ✕ deletes a row.

          💭 Check-in

          👟 Steps, 😴 hours of sleep (type 7:30, 7.5 or 7h30), and how your day felt. Pressing Enter or clicking away sets the day's steps or sleep; press the + next to the box instead to add what you typed, like a walk's steps or a nap. Pick a mood from 😣 to 😄 (tap it again to clear), and any feelings that fit. They're grouped by kind: 😊 content, ⚡ driven, 😬 tense, 🌧️ down and 🪫 drained. Everything saves on its own.

          ⚖️ Weight and body fat

          Also in the check-in: your weight in kg and body fat in %, whenever you measure them. Below them you'll see the change since your previous weigh-in and, if your height is set in Settings, your BMI.

          📝 Notes

          An optional space for a few words on how the day went. It saves when you click away, and days with a note show 📝 in History.

          🏃 Activity

          Choose what you did and for how long. Leave kcal empty and it's estimated for you from your latest logged body weight (the box shows the guess). Types you use often appear at the top.

          📊 Summary

          Calories and macros against that day's targets. Anything over target turns amber. Net kcal is what you ate minus what you burned.

          🥫 Foods

          Your own food list. Each food has its nutrition per an amount you choose, like per 100 g or per 1 slice, and logging works out the rest.

          🔎 Got a barcode? Type or scan it and press Look up. If it's already in your list, it opens. If not, it's looked up on Open Food Facts (only the barcode is sent) and the form fills in for you to check. Not found anywhere? Copy the values from the label once, and the barcode will find it next time.

          🏷️ Diet tags: Pescatarian Ovolactovegetarian Vegetarian Vegan Kosher Lactose-free Peanut-free TACC-free. Click them above your list to filter.

          • Vegan No animal or product of animal origin.
          • Vegetarian No animal, but products of animal origin are ok, like milk and dairy products. It technically doesn't allow egg consumption.
          • Ovolactovegetarian Like vegetarian, but allows egg consumption. The "lacto" part is included because of normal everyday use, though in this definition, both vegetarians and ovolactovegetarians can consume milk and dairy products.
          • Pescatarian Like vegetarian, but allows eating fish. Technically still doesn't allow eating eggs.

          So a vegan food is also vegetarian, and a vegetarian food is also both ovolactovegetarian and pescatarian. Ticking a tag ticks the ones it implies.

          🔥 kcal: leave it empty if you don't know it yet. The food still works, counted as 0 kcal, and the list marks it with ⚠ ? until you fill it in. Entering 0 means it really has none, and it gets the Calorie-free tag on its own (you can't set that tag yourself).

          ✍️ The Brand field suggests brands you've already used.

          📏 Units: besides g and ml, use u for one piece of a pack (one egg of a carton, one cookie) and whole for one whole item (an apple).

          🗂️ Groups: give each food one group (Fruits, Cookies, Drinks…). Your list has one table per group, with foods without one last; the menu above it shows a single group.

          ✅ After saving, the name of the food shows next to the form's title: click it to jump to its row.

          ↕️ Click a column title to sort every table by it. Click again to reverse, and a third time to go back to the usual order (most used first). Hide all tags makes the list more compact.

          ⚡ Scanning many products? If a barcode is already in your list, its name shows with an Edit link instead of opening it, so you can scan the next one right away.

          🧮 A hint shows the calories your macros add up to, which helps catch typos.

          🔒 Editing or deleting a food never changes days you already logged.

          ⭐ Saved meals

          Combos you eat often, like your usual breakfast. Build one here, or press ☆ save on a meal in the Day tab. Usually for picks the meal for you, and the tags show what all its foods have in common.

          📈 History

          Calories, protein, steps, sleep, body weight and body fat for the last 7, 30 or 90 days. The dashed line is your target, and it steps up or down where a target changed. Click a bar or a row to jump to that day.

          🫙 The cards on top fill up as you get closer to the target (or, for days logged and active days, to the whole period; for mood, to 😄). Hover one to see the %. Average calories or protein more than 10% above target turn the card amber.

          ⚖️ Latest weight and body fat show ▲ up, – no change or ▼ down, compared with the latest value logged before the period. Hover for the exact change.

          ⚙️ Settings

          🎯 Daily targets: press the ✏️ pencil to change one. Use a single value for every day, or custom periods if your goal changed over time. Each day uses the target from its own period, so old days keep their old goals. Going back to a single value deletes the periods (you'll be asked first).

          🧍 Profile: your gender and height (cm). Height is used for BMI; gender is only stored and isn't used in any calculation.

          🏋️ Exercise types: about 75 built in, from CrossFit to padel. Add your own or adjust how many calories each one burns. Each type can have its own emoji; leave it empty and it uses its group's (🏃 cardio, 🏋️ gym & strength, 🧘 mind & body, 🥊 classes & combat, 🏅 sports, 🏞️ outdoors & water, 🏡 everyday, and 💪 for groups you make up).

          🚪 Closing Pamela

          Press Exit in the top right corner and confirm. Pamela waits for anything still being saved, tidies up its database file and stops its server, so nothing is left running in the background. Your browser may not let a page close its own tab; if the tab stays open, just close it.

          Everything you've added is already saved as you go, so if the window closes by accident you won't lose anything. Exit is simply the clean way to finish. To start again, run make serve.

          🗂️ Your setup

          Meal names still live in info/config.toml, along with a fallback body weight used for calorie estimates until you log your first weigh-in. Edit the file, come back to this tab, and the changes show up. No restart needed. If something in it is wrong, a red banner at the top tells you what.
