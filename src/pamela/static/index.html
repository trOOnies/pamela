          New exercise type

          MET is the energy an activity takes compared with sitting still (1 MET ≈ 1 kcal per kg of body weight per hour). Look up values in the Compendium of Physical Activities.

          Day

          Log what you eat and the activities you do. Use the arrows or date picker in the top bar to move between days, or [ / ].

          Logging food from the catalog

          1. Pick the meal (it defaults to the one for the current time of day).
          2. Type in Food to search, then ↑ / ↓ and Enter to choose.
          3. Type the amount and press Enter. The preview below the form shows the macros you're adding.

          When a food has a serving or package size, buttons below the amount fill in 1 serving, ½ pack and so on; you can always type any amount.

          You can also type or scan a barcode into Food. If the food isn't in the catalog yet, choose the Create / Look up barcode option at the end of the list. It opens the Foods tab with the name or barcode filled in.

          Saved meals

          Switch to Saved meal and pick one: its foods appear as a draft. Nothing is logged until you press Log, so you can change amounts, remove foods or add others for today. The saved meal itself never changes from here.

          Quick add

          Switch to Quick add to type kcal and macros directly, without a catalog food. Good for one-offs like a restaurant meal.

          Editing the log

          Click an amount to change it (Enter saves, Esc cancels). Use + add on a meal heading to log straight into that meal, ☆ save to keep its foods as a saved meal, and ✕ to delete a row.

          Check-in

          Steps, hours of sleep (type 7:30, 7.5 or 7h30) and mood, with any feelings that apply. They save as soon as you change them.

          Activity

          Choose a type and enter the minutes. If you leave kcal empty it's estimated as MET × body weight (kg) × hours; the placeholder shows the estimate. Distance is asked only for types that use it. Types are grouped by category, with the ones you've used lately at the top; manage them in Settings.

          Summary

          Shows kcal and each macro against your daily targets. Values over target turn amber. Net kcal is eaten minus burned.

          Foods

          Your catalog. Each food stores its macros per a basis amount, e.g. per 100 g or per 1 slice; logging scales them to the amount you eat.

          While you fill in the form, a hint shows the kcal your macros add up to (4 kcal/g protein and carbs, 9 kcal/g fat), handy for catching typos.

          Barcode: type or scan the EAN and press Look up. If it's already in your catalog, that food opens for editing. Otherwise it's searched on Open Food Facts (only the barcode is sent) and the form is filled in for you to check and save. If it isn't found, enter the label's values: the food is saved with its barcode, so typing or scanning it later finds it.

          Diet tags (vegetarian, vegan, kosher, lactose-free, peanut-free, TACC-free) show as colored labels; click them above the catalog to filter. A saved meal shows the tags all its foods share. Vegan implies vegetarian.

          Foods you've logged most in the last 90 days are listed (and suggested) first. Editing or deleting a food never changes past log entries: each entry keeps a copy of the macros it was logged with.

          Saved meals

          A fixed mix of catalog foods you eat often, like a usual breakfast. Build one here, or press ☆ save on a meal in the Day tab. Usually for preselects the meal slot when you use it. Logging a saved meal copies its foods into the day, so editing those entries (or the saved meal later) doesn't affect the other.

          History

          Daily kcal, protein, steps and sleep over the last 7, 30 or 90 days, with your target as a dashed line. Averages count only days with data. Click a bar or a row to open that day.

          Your settings

          These come from info/config.toml. Edit that file and switch back to this tab to see the changes; no restart needed. If the file has a mistake, a red banner at the top explains it. Exercise types have moved into the app: Settings.
