      Day

      Log what you eat and the activities you do. Use the arrows or date picker in the top bar to move between days, or [ / ].

      Logging food from the catalog

      1. Pick the meal (it defaults to the one for the current time of day).
      2. Type in Food to search, then ↑ / ↓ and Enter to choose.
      3. Type the amount and press Enter. The preview below the form shows the macros you're adding.

      If the food isn't in the catalog yet, choose the Create option at the end of the list. It opens the Foods tab with the name filled in.

      Quick add

      Switch to Quick add to type kcal and macros directly, without a catalog food. Good for one-offs like a restaurant meal.

      Editing the log

      Click an amount to change it (Enter saves, Esc cancels). Use + add on a meal heading to log straight into that meal, and ✕ to delete a row.

      Activity

      Choose a type and enter the minutes. If you leave kcal empty it's estimated as MET × body weight (kg) × hours; the placeholder shows the estimate. Distance is asked only for types that use it.

      Summary

      Shows kcal and each macro against your daily targets. Values over target turn amber. Net kcal is eaten minus burned.

      Foods

      Your catalog. Each food stores its macros per a basis amount, e.g. per 100 g or per 1 slice; logging scales them to the amount you eat.

      While you fill in the form, a hint shows the kcal your macros add up to (4 kcal/g protein and carbs, 9 kcal/g fat), handy for catching typos.

      Foods you've logged most in the last 90 days are listed (and suggested) first. Editing or deleting a food never changes past log entries: each entry keeps a copy of the macros it was logged with.

      History

      Daily kcal and protein over the last 7, 30 or 90 days, with your target as a dashed line. Calorie and macro averages count only days with food logged. Click a bar or a row to open that day.

      Keyboard

      Your settings

      These come from info/config.toml. Edit that file and switch back to this tab to see the changes; no restart needed. If the file has a mistake, a red banner at the top explains it.
