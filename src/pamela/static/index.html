                  Welcome to Pamela

                  Your food and fitness diary, under a sun hat. 🌞

                  Pamela is your personal food and fitness diary. Each day you can log what you eat, how you move, how you slept and how you feel, and then look back over weeks and months to see how it all adds up.

                  🏠 It's all yours, on your computer. Pamela runs on your own machine and keeps everything in a single file, local/pamela.sqlite3. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. The only time it goes online is when you look up a barcode, and then only the barcode itself is sent.

                  💾 No save button needed. Everything you add or change is saved the moment you do it.

                  🗺️ Finding your way. The tabs at the top are the parts of the app:

                  • Day: your diary for one day. This is where you'll spend most of your time.
                  • Foods: your own list of foods and their nutrition.
                  • Groceries: what you have at home, what you bought and what expired.
                  • Fridge: your groceries sorted by where you keep them at home.
                  • Meals: combos you eat often, ready to log in one go, with their recipes.
                  • History: charts and totals over 7, 30 or 90 days.
                  • Settings: your targets, profile and exercise types.
                  • Help: this page. Press ? to come back any time.

                  ☀️ Light or dark? The sun / moon button next to Exit switches between the two. Pamela starts out matching your system's setting, and remembers your choice in this browser.

                  When you're done, press 🚪 Exit in the top right corner to close Pamela properly.

                  📅 Day

                  This is where you log your day: what you eat, how you move, and how you feel. Use the arrows or the date at the top to change day, or press [ and ]. Dates are shown as year-month-day. The Today button is highlighted when you're on today, and days in the future can't be opened yet.

                  🍽️ Logging a food

                  1. Pick the meal. It already guesses one from the time of day.
                  2. Start typing the food's name, brand or barcode, then choose it with ↑ ↓ and Enter.
                  3. Type how much you had and press Enter. The line under the form shows what you're adding.

                  🥄 Had only part of it? If the food has a serving or package size, tap 1 serving, ⅙ pack, ⅓ pack, ½ pack and so on. You can always type any amount.

                  🆕 Not in your foods yet? Pick Create (or Look up barcode) at the end of the list, and the Foods tab opens with it filled in.

                  ⭐ Saved meals

                  Switch to Saved meal and pick one. Its foods show up as a draft: change amounts, remove things, add extras. Nothing is logged until you press Log, and the saved meal itself stays exactly as it was.

                  ⚡ Quick add

                  For one-offs, like a restaurant dish: type a name and the calories and macros you know.

                  ✏️ Fixing the log

                  Click an amount to change it (Enter saves, Esc cancels). + add logs straight into that meal, ☆ save keeps its foods as a saved meal, and ✕ deletes a row.

                  🔗 next to a row opens where it came from: the meal in the Meals tab if you logged it from one, otherwise the food in the Foods tab. Quick adds have no link.

                  💭 Check-in

                  👟 Steps, 😴 hours of sleep (type 7:30, 7.5 or 7h30), and how your day felt. Pressing Enter or clicking away sets the day's steps or sleep; press the + next to the box instead to add what you typed, like a walk's steps or a nap. Pick a mood from 😣 to 😄 (tap it again to clear), and any feelings that fit. They're grouped by kind: 😊 content, ⚡ driven, 😬 tense, 🌧️ down and 🪫 drained. Everything saves on its own.

                  🌙 From 9 PM to midnight, today's check-in reminds you to log your steps before the day ends.

                  ⚖️ Weight and body fat

                  Also in the check-in: your weight in kg and body fat in %, whenever you measure them. Below them you'll see the change since your previous weigh-in and, if your height is set in Settings, your BMI.

                  📝 Notes

                  An optional space for a few words on how the day went. It saves when you click away, and days with a note show 📝 in History.

                  🏃 Activity

                  Choose what you did and for how long. Leave kcal empty and it's estimated for you from your latest logged body weight (the box shows the guess). Types you use often appear at the top.

                  🌤️ Open Conditions to add, if you like: the time of day, temperature (°C), humidity (%), wind (km/h) and how comfortable it felt, from ★ to ★★★★★ (tap the same star again to clear it). Activities with a time are listed in time order. Press the ✏️ pencil on a logged activity to change it or add its conditions later.

                  🗑️ Clearing a day

                  Clear this day, below the notes, deletes everything logged on the day you're looking at: its food, activities, check-in and note, as if it had never been filled in. What its food took from Groceries goes back. You're asked first, and it can't be undone. Your foods and saved meals stay.

                  📊 Summary

                  Calories and macros against that day's targets. Anything over target turns amber. Net kcal is what you ate minus what you burned.

                  👟 Burned and Active include your steps, counted as easy walking (about 100 steps a minute). Steps that a logged walk, run or hike already accounts for aren't counted twice: those types have a number of steps per minute in Settings, and only the steps beyond them are added. The line under the summary shows what the steps added.

                  🥫 Foods

                  Your own food list. Each food has its nutrition per an amount you choose, like per 100 g or per 1 slice, and logging works out the rest. To compare foods easily, the list always shows foods in g or ml per 100 g / 100 ml, whatever amount you entered them with.

                  🔎 Got a barcode? Type or scan it and press Look up. If it's already in your list, it opens. If not, it's looked up on Open Food Facts (only the barcode is sent) and the form fills in for you to check. Not found anywhere? Copy the values from the label once, and the barcode will find it next time.

                  🏷️ Diet tags: Pescatarian Ovolactovegetarian Vegetarian Vegan Kosher Lactose-free Peanut-free TACC-free. Click them above your list to filter.

                  • Vegan No animal or product of animal origin.
                  • Vegetarian No animal, but products of animal origin are ok, like milk and dairy products. It technically doesn't allow egg consumption.
                  • Ovolactovegetarian Like vegetarian, but allows egg consumption. The "lacto" part is included because of normal everyday use, though in this definition, both vegetarians and ovolactovegetarians can consume milk and dairy products.
                  • Pescatarian Like vegetarian, but allows eating fish. Technically still doesn't allow eating eggs.

                  So a vegan food is also vegetarian, and a vegetarian food is also both ovolactovegetarian and pescatarian. Ticking a tag ticks the ones it implies.

                  🔥 kcal: leave it empty if you don't know it yet. The food still works, counted as 0 kcal, and the list marks it with ⚠ ? until you fill it in. Entering 0 means it really has none, and it gets the Calorie-free tag on its own (you can't set that tag yourself).

                  ✍️ The Brand field suggests brands you've already used.

                  📏 Units: besides g and ml, use u for one piece of a pack (one egg of a carton, one cookie) and whole for one whole item (an apple).

                  🗂️ Groups: give each food one group (Fruits, Cookies, Drinks…). Your list has one table per group, with foods without one last; the menu above it shows a single group.

                  ✅ After saving, the name of the food shows next to the form's title: click it to jump to its row.

                  ↕️ Click a column title to sort every table by it. Click again to reverse, and a third time to go back to the usual order (most used first). Hide all tags makes the list more compact.

                  ⚡ Scanning many products? If a barcode is already in your list, its name shows with an Edit link instead of opening it, so you can scan the next one right away.

                  🧮 A hint shows the calories your macros add up to, which helps catch typos.

                  🔄 Editing a food updates every day you logged it on, with its new values. Deleting a food leaves logged days as they are.

                  🛒 Groceries

                  What you have at home, in carts: one per trip to the shops. Back from the shops? Under New cart, set the day and the store, add each food you bought (from your Foods), and press Add cart. For each food you can give the amount (or the number of packs, if the food has a package size), its expiry date and what it cost. Only the amount is needed.

                  🧴 Other items: things that aren't food, like soap or bags, go under + Add an item that isn't food with a name, amount, unit and price. They only count towards the cart's total, so it matches the receipt: they're not added to your Foods or your stock. A cart can even be only other items.

                  🏷️ Discount: type an amount ($2.50) or a percentage (10%), taken off the cart's subtotal. The form shows the subtotal, the discount and the total as you go. The discount can't be more than the subtotal.

                  💲 Money shows in dollars with cents, like $1,234.50: a comma between thousands and a point before the cents. Type prices with a point (2.50).

                  🚚 Delivery fee: had it delivered? Enter the fee, if you like. It's added to the total after the discount (the discount never applies to it).

                  🛒 Carts lists every cart, newest first, with what's left of each food and what it cost. Press ✏️ to edit one: change its day or store, amounts, dates or prices, add foods or remove them, then Save cart. An amount can't go below what was already eaten or thrown out of it. ✕ (or Delete cart while editing) deletes a cart: what's left of it leaves your stock, and logged days stay as they are. Same shopping again? The copy button makes a new cart exactly like that one, which you can then edit like any other. Each food shows its group's emoji.

                  🍽️ Eating takes from it. When you log a food in the Day tab, that amount comes out of your groceries, starting with what expires first. A 🛒 next to the amount in the log shows it. Change or delete the entry, or clear the day, and it goes back. Ate more than you had? It takes what's there; the rest isn't counted. Days before you bought it never take from it, but if you enter a cart a few days late, what you logged since that day is taken from it.

                  📦 In stock lists every food you have, one table per food group like in Foods (foods without a group last), with what's left from each cart under it (click the cart to open it). Ran out and Expired are grouped the same way. Expiry dates turn amber three days before and red once they've passed. Sort by what expires first, or A–Z.

                  🗑️ Something expired? Press Expired on it, say how much went (all that's left, unless you change it) and when. It leaves your stock and shows under Expired, where Undo puts it back.

                  🔁 Ran out lists foods you've finished, with + Buy again to put them in the cart you're filling in.

                  🔎 In the Day tab, the food search and the line under the form show how much of a food is left.

                  📍 Under each food in In stock, where it is in the Fridge tab: its sector, or each sector with its share, or not in a Fridge sector. Click it to open Fridge.

                  ⚠️ Past their date. When something in stock has passed its expiry date, a red warning on top of Groceries and Fridge names it. Click a name to jump to its row here, then press Expired if it went off, or fix the date in its cart if it's still fine.

                  📦 A food with a package size also shows how many packs are left. Less than a tenth of a pack shows as < 0.1 pack: if that looks wrong, check the amount in its cart, which is in the food's unit (ml, g…), not in packs.

                  🧊 Fridge

                  Your groceries sorted by where you keep them at home, so you can stand in front of the fridge and check that Pamela matches what's really there.

                  🗂️ Sectors. Add one for each place you keep food: fridge shelves, the door, freezer drawers, the pantry… Give each a name and, if you like, an emoji. Use ↑ and ↓ to put them in the order you walk past them, ✏️ to rename one, ✕ to delete one (its foods just go back to Not in a sector).

                  🗂️ The New sector form is at the very bottom of the page.

                  📥 Putting food in. Not in a sector lists everything in stock that has no place yet: press Put in… next to it. Or use + Add a food from your stock at the bottom of a sector. Move… moves a food to another sector, and ✕ takes it out of one.

                  ✂️ One food, many sectors. When you move or put in a food, say how much (all of it, half, one pack or any amount). Move part of it and it's in both sectors, each showing its share (300 ml of 1,000). The sector that holds the rest is its main one: new purchases of it land there. Moving all of the main sector's share makes the other sector the main one. ✕ on a share sends it back to the main sector.

                  🍽️ When you eat some or throw some out, Pamela can't know which share it came from: it takes it from the main sector first, then from the other shares, newest first. If that's not what happened, move it to match.

                  🔁 A food that runs out stays in its main sector, greyed out as none left, so it's back in its place as soon as you buy it again.

                  ✅ Checking. Tick each food once you've seen it where Pamela says it is. The count at the top shows how far you are, and a sector gets a green border when all of it is ticked. Ticks are remembered in this browser until you press Start a new check. Something missing or gone off? Fix it in Groceries (Expired) or by logging what you ate.

                  ⭐ Meals

                  Combos you eat often, like your usual breakfast. Build one here, or press ☆ save on a meal in the Day tab. Usually for picks the meal for you, and the tags show what all its foods have in common. In the Day tab they're under Saved meal.

                  📝 Recipe. Each meal can hold a recipe as free text: steps, times, tips. Open 📝 Recipe on its card to read it.

                  🛒 Can I make it? Each card compares the meal with your groceries: ✓ next to the foods you have enough of, ⚠ next to the ones you're short of, and a line saying either You have everything to make it or what's Missing and how much. + Buy missing puts what's missing in the cart in Groceries (in whole packs when the food has a package size). Tick Only what I can make now to see just the meals you can cook with what's at home.

                  📈 History

                  Calories, protein, steps, sleep, body weight and body fat for the last 7, 30 or 90 days. The dashed line is your target, and it steps up or down where a target changed. Click a bar or a row to jump to that day.

                  🫙 The cards on top fill up as you get closer to the target (or, for days logged and active days, to the whole period; for mood, to 😄). Hover one to see the %. Average calories or protein more than 10% above target turn the card amber.

                  ⚖️ Latest weight and body fat show ▲ up, – no change or ▼ down, compared with the latest value logged before the period. Hover for the exact change.

                  ⚖️ kg or BMI: the switch on the body weight chart shows your weight in kg or as BMI (body mass index: kg divided by your height in meters, squared). BMI needs your height, set in Settings.

                  📐 Weight references, below that chart, split weight into six brackets, from Underweight to Severely obese, each with the body type it's usually associated with (e.g. Lean, Curvy, Stocky). They follow the switch, in kg or BMI, and your latest weight's bracket is highlighted. They're defined in BMI, so they also need your height.

                  The default values follow a stereotypical Western idea of body shapes, with one set for men, one for women and one for everyone else (from your gender in Settings). They're a rough guide, not a medical assessment: you can set your own in Settings.

                  ⚙️ Settings

                  🎯 Daily targets: press the ✏️ pencil to change one. Use a single value for every day, or custom periods if your goal changed over time. Each day uses the target from its own period, so old days keep their old goals. Going back to a single value deletes the periods (you'll be asked first).

                  🧍 Profile: your gender and height (cm). Height is used for BMI; gender picks the default weight references (male, female or other) and isn't used in any calculation.

                  📐 Weight references: the five BMI values where each bracket starts, from Slim to Severely obese. Each must be above 0 and higher than the one before. With your height set, the kg each one means shows next to it. Fill in the defaults for men, women or other and adjust from there, or go back to your gender's defaults.

                  🏋️ Exercise types: about 75 built in, from CrossFit to padel. Add your own or adjust how many calories each one burns. Each type can have its own emoji; leave it empty and it uses its group's (🏃 cardio, 🏋️ gym & strength, 🧘 mind & body, 🥊 classes & combat, 🏅 sports, 🏞️ outdoors & water, 🏡 everyday, and 💪 for groups you make up). Types done on foot (walking, running, hiking…) have the steps they take per minute, so your day's steps don't count them twice; leave it empty for the rest.

                  ⚠️ Danger Zone, at the bottom: tick what to delete for good (food log, activities, check-ins, day notes, meals, fridge sectors, groceries, food catalog), or Select all, and press Delete. To confirm, type delete (or delete all when everything is ticked). Deleting the food catalog keeps the values of days you logged, but empties meals made of those foods. Settings are never deleted.

                  🚪 Closing Pamela

                  Press Exit in the top right corner and confirm. Pamela waits for anything still being saved, tidies up its database file and stops its server, so nothing is left running in the background. Your browser may not let a page close its own tab; if the tab stays open, just close it.

                  Everything you've added is already saved as you go, so if the window closes by accident you won't lose anything. Exit is simply the clean way to finish. To start again, run make serve.

                  🗂️ Your setup

                  Meal names still live in info/config.toml, along with a fallback body weight used for calorie estimates until you log your first weigh-in. Edit the file, come back to this tab, and the changes show up. No restart needed. If something in it is wrong, a red banner at the top tells you what.
